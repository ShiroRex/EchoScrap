## Prompt para Meshy

Low-poly stylized medieval garage fusion machine, matching the reference image. Freestanding blacksmith workshop device, dark wrought iron frame, bronze rivets, dark oak panels and stone feet. Two large upper input hoppers connected by thick copper pipes to a central round fusion chamber. Segmented metal iris door with a small purple emissive core, wide lower output tray, large side crank lever, analog pressure gauge, two short chimneys. Chunky beveled forms, readable silhouette, restrained hand-painted wear, simple PBR materials. Approximately 4 m wide, 3 m tall, 2 m deep. Fully modeled front, sides and back; flat stable base. Game-ready low-poly geometry, clean UVs, no floating or disconnected parts. Keep body, iris door, crank and output tray as separate objects if supported, for animation in Unity. No text, logos, characters, environment, smoke, particles or baked shadows. No tiny wires or fragile details.
